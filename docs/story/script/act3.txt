aldous.neutral: Where did you learn that song, Sister?
hesper.neutral: I don't know. I've always known it. Why?

tamsin.neutral: Hm.

tamsin.neutral: I held his hand at the end. Outside, in the mud. I told him I'd make the one who did it kneel. I swore it.
tamsin.neutral: That northern woman says that counts. That I've been carrying an oath two years and never knew.
bren.neutral: Does it feel like it counts?
tamsin.sad: It feels like a stone in my chest that gets heavier every winter.
bren.neutral: The captain. Stroud.
tamsin.angry: She gave the order. I saw her.
bren.neutral: Jory's village had Hollows too. Never broke an oath. Nobody burned it. It just... owed.
tamsin.neutral: What are you saying?
bren.neutral: Nothing yet. Only that I'm not sure who it is that ought to be kneeling.
tamsin.smirk: ...I hate it when you think, Lantern. It's very annoying.
jory.neutral: Writing again, Eira? Who to?
eira.neutral: Home.
jory.neutral: Long way, the north. Who's at home?
eira.neutral: My father. He likes to hear what I learn.
jory.smirk: Proud of you, is he?
eira.neutral: He is... attentive. || A very carefully chosen word.
eira.neutral: How many marks are on your arms now, Jory?
jory.neutral: Two hundred and twelve. Two hundred and thirteen after tomorrow, if the Witness is kind.
eira.neutral: And if it is not?
jory.smirk: Then somebody else can do the counting.
hesper.neutral: Bren.
bren.neutral: Can't sleep?
hesper.neutral: No.
hesper.neutral: What if it's about me? The box. Odo looked at me, before he died. He said it wasn't my fault. Why would he say that?
bren.neutral: Because it isn't. Whatever it is.
hesper.sad: You don't know what it is.
bren.neutral: Doesn't matter.
hesper.angry: It does matter! What if I'm... what if I'm something terrible, Bren? What if that's why Morrow chose me, why the ink won't take... || The fear she has been holding for three days, all at once.
bren.neutral: Hess.
bren.neutral: Whatever they've written about you in that box, you're still the girl who put frogs in my bed and corrected my spelling when I carved my name in the chapel door.
hesper.smirk: You spelled Holloway with one "l".
bren.smirk: It's a fair spelling.
hesper.sad: And if it changes everything?
bren.neutral: Then it changes everything. You'll still be you. I'll know. I'm the witness, remember?
hesper.neutral: ...Whatever they write about us.
bren.neutral: We keep each other's truth.
